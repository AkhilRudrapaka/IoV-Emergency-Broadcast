# Audit and reconciliation (2026-11-03)

**Sources.** Four independent auditors (radio/forwarding `RAD`, metrics/experiment `MET`, scenario/trust/security `SCE`,
demo/viewer/docs `DEM`) produced the evidence-backed findings saved verbatim in
[`audit/auditor_reports.txt`](audit/auditor_reports.txt). The four planned **skeptic reviews did not run**: every one
failed with an account usage limit, so no adversarial verification exists. In its place, the "My check" column says
how each claim acted on was checked in this session: **code** = re-read the cited lines; **measured** = reproduced
with my own run, probe or screenshot; **auditor only** = not independently re-checked (only used for documentation, never as
the sole basis for a code change). Spot-checks S1–S4 are the four findings the project owner asked to verify first.

Status legend: **DEFECT** confirmed, fix it · **LIMIT** confirmed modelling limitation, keep it and disclose it · **OPT** optional
improvement, deferred · **OK** works as documented, keep it.

## 3. Confirmed limitations (kept, disclosed with measurements)

| ID | Limitation | Evidence | Why kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCE-1 | **Fail-closed corroboration**: without 3 reputable votes a genuine alert is never released (no timeout) | Auditor: 0/180 runs at 300 m for the full scheme; 14/30 at d50 with 150 m range | It is a deliberate security choice (no amplification without corroboration). The rate is reported by the `accepted` metric. A quorum-timeout local fallback is specified in SYSTEM_DESIGN.md §5 as next work |
| SCE-3 | Forged-alert protection comes from the reputation gate, not from voting + BLS alone | Auditor: with on-off attackers (trust 0.89), the forged alert was registered in 8/10 runs | Disclosed. The on-off sweep (S2) measures it |
| SCE-5 (keys) | Keys derived from vehicle IDs, no PKI or proof of possession | `corroboration.py:36-37` | Simulation shortcut, disclosed |
| SCE-10 | Incident is staged on a (possibly moving) vehicle; the experiment does not stop it | `scenario.py:119, 144` | The dissemination uses one snapshot (≤ 1.25 m movement in 64 ms, SCE-7). The demo now stops the crashed car |
| SCE-11, SCE-13 | Small straight 5×5 grid (3–5 hops); clustering yields ~1 CH per cluster | Auditor measurements | Disclosed. A larger map is future work |
| SCE-15 | RSU location check is a bounding box; in simulated runs the time and hop checks never trigger (no replays are simulated) | `rsu.py:42-49` | Location kept as a sanity check. The time check now works on the RSU clock (NEW-1) and is unit-tested |
| RAD-4, RAD-5, RAD-6 | Slotted1P loses reach in 2-D at low density; at 150 m Proposed hits HOP_LIMIT; reach denominator ignores hop limit and black holes | Auditor measurements | Disclosed next to the results |
| RAD-10 | Most "collision losses" hit vehicles that already had the alert | Auditor: 3,035 of 9,509 harmful at d200_r02 | Narrative corrected; the metric definition was already accurate |
| RAD-12 | ch_route picks routes by distance even if a head cannot reach quorum | Auditor: 1/30 runs | Documented failure mode |
| MET-7 | `t_last_ms` / `mean_delay_ms` cover reached vehicles only | `metrics.py:106-107` | Always reported next to reachability |
